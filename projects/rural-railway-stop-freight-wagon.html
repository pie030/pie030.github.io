01 / The environment

One working railway.
Not disconnected props.

Rails sit on sleepers and ballast, aligned with the wagon wheels. A platform runs beside the track, leading to a station with a covered entrance. Eight conifers, four broadleaf trees, shrubs, rocks, benches and telegraph poles give the small stop its rural setting. The trees were rebuilt in v1.1 to improve their silhouettes and connection to the terrain.

02 / Nature refinement · v1.1

Recognizable trees.
Grounded in the landscape.

The first version used detached conical foliage and placed some trunks beyond the terrain. Version 1.1 rebuilds the woodland with continuous conifer crowns, branched broadleaf trees and better-grounded undergrowth.

All 12 trees now stand inside the terrain boundary. Shrubs, grasses, fencing and the access path were adjusted to avoid disconnected or floating-looking elements. The revision adds 420 triangles while preserving the station, wagon and track geometry.

03 / Design decisions

Engineering clarity.
Low-poly discipline.

The refinement focused on physical connections, recognizable mechanisms and structural completeness.

01 / Architecture

Closed and connected.

The station has solid roof panels, gable infills, framed openings and a threshold linking the front entrance to the platform.

02 / Rolling stock

Wheels meet rails.

The freight wagon uses two axles, four low-poly flanged wheels, suspension brackets, sliding cargo doors, buffers and couplings. Both wagon roof ends were closed during refinement.

03 / Site layout

Respect the terrain.

The terrain was extended beyond the track ends to prevent floating sleepers. The platform, track, station and rural landscape share a consistent coordinate system.

04 / Technical notes

Actual geometry.
Documented limits.

The base geometry was generated with Python tools, later refined in Blender and exported as glTF 2.0. These images are renders of the actual model, not concept art. The file is a static visual study, not an operational railway simulation.

The complete Y-up GLB (losslessly packed for web delivery) and four modular exports share the same origin. Geometry was reimported and checked for invalid vertices, degenerate triangles, roof closure and modular consistency. The v1.1 review also checked that all 12 tree roots are within the island, and that the station and wagon meshes remain unchanged.
