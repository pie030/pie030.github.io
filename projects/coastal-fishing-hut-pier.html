02 / Design decisions

Readable structure.
Lean geometry.

The project was refined around recognizable construction rather than adding detail for its own sake.

01 / Architecture

Close every surface.

Both roof slopes are solid panels with thickness and closed gables. Door and window openings are modeled in the wall geometry, not painted on a flat facade.

02 / Maritime objects

Build for function.

The pier deck rests on stringers and piles extending below the waterline. The rowboat has a tapered, open hull, internal seats, oars, and a mooring line.

03 / Refinement

Remove visual noise.

The beach edge was rebuilt as a continuous contour rather than disconnected triangles. Small fish silhouettes and material-based mesh consolidation improved legibility and efficiency.

03 / Technical notes

Modeling and refinement.

The base geometry was generated with Python geometry tools and subsequently refined in Blender. The images show the exported model, not concept art. This is a static 3D environment study, not a playable game.

The final GLB was exported with Y-up coordinates. A full-scene file and four modular files are included in the development package; the GLB below is the complete model.

Geometry was re-imported and checked for finite vertices, degenerate triangles, closed roof surfaces and consistency between modular exports.
